vanille is an app designed mainly for me. 

Like most people, when I have an idea for a project, it doesn't all come at the same time. It comes in bits and pieces I end up either forgetting, scribbling down then never looking at again, or even talking to ChatGPT about it, just to forget it even existed.

I realized <b>THAT</b> was the biggest thing holding me back, simply being disorganized. So why not build something to organize for me.

That's where vanille comes in. The biggest thing I wanted was a way to easily write down something and have other systems classify it in the right place for me.

The goal is to have vanille automatically find relevant information in your inputs and index it in a way that's easily queryable or navigatable for future use. 

I envision vanille to have two modes:
 1. Listen: talk about your project, what you want it to be some ideas you have and vanille automatically extracts important information.
 
 2. Active: talk with a ChatGPT like assistant about your project, debate about features and expand further and further until you have a design, the agent has access to your vanille       db and can recall and make references to some of your other ideas, and help you find links you never would've thought of.
